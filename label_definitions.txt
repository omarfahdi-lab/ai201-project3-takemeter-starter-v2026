backed: makes a claim and supports it with at least one stated fact a reader could check, such as a number, date, price, sales figure, quote, link, or a specific thing that happened, offered as evidence rather than as the topic
bare_take: states an opinion, verdict or prediction as if it were true, with no checkable fact offered in support, including sarcasm whose point is a clear verdict
reaction: expresses a feeling, a joke, or a jab without asserting any claim about a game, company or the industry that someone could argue against
